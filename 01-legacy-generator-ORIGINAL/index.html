Identity
Design

Contrast is checked automatically against the WCAG AA threshold. No drop shadows, no decorative gradients, no cards, no emoji.

The accent is for decoration only — the eyebrow chip and other details. It is never used for small text: the pale sets are unreadable as text on a light background.

Text size scales the whole type scale of the generated site, headings included. Line length stays optimal.

Finishing

To adjust the rendering. These settings are already correct by default.

Home page

Sections: a title and some text. Minimal Markdown accepted — `## Title`, `- list`, `**bold**`, `[link](url)`.

Pages

No pages yet. The site works with the home page alone, but it needs at least one page to be reachable.

Tick "Show the contact form" on one page: that is where the form is inserted, and it is the site's only point of contact. The legal pages (privacy, terms) are generated separately and cannot be edited here.

Images

Recompressed locally in your browser (WebP, resized to max 1600 px wide). Before/after weight is shown. The share image and the favicon are generated from your site name.

Contact

The anti-spam field is an invisible honeypot plus a 3 s minimum delay. No CAPTCHA, no third-party service, therefore no script loaded from anywhere else.

Legal pages

These two pages are generated automatically, with no template to fill in. Leave these fields blank and the site name and contact address are used instead. Fill them with your real information before publishing.

Settings

No Google Analytics, no remote Matomo, no tracking pixel: those tools send your IP to a third party. Self-hosted Plausible and Umami count without cookies and without sending anything to a company. A consent banner is generated only if you enable one.
